# Shreem Farms Brand Search Playbook

Goal: make Google understand `Shreem Farms` as the exact brand/entity, so it stops treating it as a typo for `Shree Farms`.

## Code Signals Already Expected

- Homepage title, description, Open Graph site name, PWA manifest, visible H1, footer, and JSON-LD should use `Shreem Farms` as the primary brand.
- `Shreem`, `Shreem Cow Products`, and `shreemfarms` should be alternate names, not the primary name.
- Sitemap should include the root domain and `/in` homepage.
- Product pages should keep canonical URLs on `https://www.shreemfarms.in`.

## Search Console Steps

1. Inspect `https://www.shreemfarms.in/`, `https://www.shreemfarms.in/in`, and key product URLs.
2. Request indexing after deployment.
3. Submit `https://www.shreemfarms.in/sitemap.xml`.
4. Track exact queries:
   - `shreem farms`
   - `shreemfarms`
   - `shreem farms ghee`
   - `shreem farms bilona ghee`
   - `shreem farms astrology`

## External Entity Signals

Use the exact spelling `Shreem Farms` everywhere.

- Google Business Profile name: `Shreem Farms`
- Instagram/Facebook/YouTube/Pinterest bio: include `Shreem Farms` and `shreemfarms.in`.
- Directory/citation listings: use the same brand, website, phone, address, and description.
- Invoice/order emails: sender name and footer should say `Shreem Farms`.
- Product labels, PDFs, and downloaded Kundli reports should include `Shreem Farms`.
- Blog author/publisher should be `Shreem Farms`.

## Backlink Priorities

- Google Business Profile posts linking to product pages.
- Local directories and farm/natural food directories.
- YouTube video descriptions for bilona ghee, gau-kasht cooking, neem dhoop, Jeevamrut, and Jyotish.
- Pinterest pins for product visuals and blog images.
- Guest posts or interviews with natural farming, D2C food, Ayurveda, pooja, and gaushala communities.

## Content Priorities

Create/update pages that repeatedly but naturally use the exact brand:

- `About Shreem Farms`
- `Shreem Farms A2 Bilona Ghee`
- `Shreem Farms Neem Dhoop`
- `Shreem Farms Natural Farming Inputs`
- `Shreem Farms Jyotish`

Avoid stuffing. The goal is entity confidence, not repetition.
